GIF encode + decode.

wtfgif is a small, fast GIF encoder and decoder for the browser and Node. The encoder race uses arbitrary RGBA images, initializes WebAssembly before timing, and checks the resulting GIF pixels on this device. Encode-only apps can import wtfgif/encode to skip the decoder runtime.
